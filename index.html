I study the mechanics of soft dissipative solids at large strains (elastomers, filled rubber, soft biological and magneto-active materials) by combining continuum thermodynamics, mechanical testing and computation. My recent work embeds neural networks inside thermodynamically admissible rheological models, implemented in an open-source finite-element framework built on FEniCSx, and develops viscoelastic phase-field models of rate-dependent fracture in elastomers. Much of this work is carried out with industry, from the testing and modelling of filled rubber to magnetorheological elastomers for vibration damping.
